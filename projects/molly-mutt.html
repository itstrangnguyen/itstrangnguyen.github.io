Overview

Molly Mutt Europe sells dog bed covers that owners fill with their old clothes, blankets and cushions, so the bed smells like home and no synthetic stuffing goes to landfill. The covers are certified non-toxic (Oeko-Tex Standard 100, GOTS for the organic line) and machine washable. The brand comes from California and was named one of NYT Wirecutter’s Best Dog Beds.

In a group of three, we planned a paid Meta campaign that tests which message brings eco-minded dog owners in Germany to the website. Alongside the group work, I built reusable content for the brand's organic channels.
