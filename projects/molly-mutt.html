Project overview

(W.I.P) Molly Mutt EU

Molly Mutt Europe sells dog bed covers that owners fill with their own old textiles. This project has two parts: a €300 Meta Ads test plan we built as a team, and creative assets I made on my own for the brand.

Disclaimer: Molly Mutt Europe is the partner company in my DCI course. All brand assets belong to Molly Mutt Europe and appear with their permission. The team had no access to the brand's analytics or internal tools, so all figures are plans or estimates.

Client
Molly Mutt Europe (DCI partner)
Format
Teamwork (Part 1) + own work (Part 2)
Time
~2 weeks
Tools
Meta Ads Manager, Canva, Google Flow, Claude

Two parts

In a group of three, we planned a paid Meta campaign that tests which message brings eco-minded dog owners in Germany to the website. Alongside the group work, I built reusable content for the brand's organic channels. Each part has its own page: Part 1 is group work with my own pieces marked, Part 2 is entirely my own.
