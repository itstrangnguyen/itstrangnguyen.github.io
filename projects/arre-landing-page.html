Arre Mexican Essen: Team Cooking Event

A lead-generation landing page for a Mexican caterer in Berlin. Companies request a quote for a team cooking event through one short form.

About this project: About this project: Arre Mexican Essen is a real business in Berlin-Charlottenburg. This is an independent course project: I had no contact with the owner, and the page was not launched for the business. The reviews are quoted from Arre’s public website; the price and response time are my own assumptions.

The brief

Arre’s course page invites visitors to get in touch but offers no form, no phone number and no price. My SEO audit of the site flagged this as the main gap.

A company planning a team event might find Arre through a Google ad for “Teamevent Kochkurs Berlin” or a social post. That click shouldn’t land on the full website, with its menu, catering offers and blog. It should land on one page with one offer and one action: request a quote.

Goal

Companies request a quote for a Mexican cooking class as a team event.

Type

Lead generation: one page, one offer, one action.

Target

5% of visitors request a quote (my assumption).

Why a quote, not a consultation call? Arre can only name a price once it knows the date, group size and venue, and the organiser wants that price before committing to a call.

Audience

Arre’s course page already pitches team building, so I wrote for the person who organises the event, not the guests. The persona is my assumption, based on Arre’s offers and reviews.

Sandra, 34

Office manager at a Berlin company. Plans the team event for 12 colleagues, with a fixed budget per person.

Wants

  • An activity where everyone join
  • Little planning effort
  • One invoice for the company

Worries

  • Whether the menu suits everyone’s diet
  • What it costs per person
  • Whether her date is free

Sandra searches “Teamevent Kochkurs Berlin”. The ad targets that search, and the page’s headline, title and URL repeat it, so the page matches the ad she clicked.

What I built

One wireframe, three views: how the page is structured, how the quote form works, and which colours and type it uses. Switch the view with the tabs, then hover over or tap a marker.

The landing page

Limits of this case study

  • I had no contact with the owner, so the price stays a placeholder: „ab [€__] pro Person”.
  • “Angebot in 24 Stunden” is my assumption, not a promise from Arre.
  • The three reviews describe catering for companies, not the cooking course. A note under the cards says so.
  • The photos come from Unsplash, not from Arre's courses.
  • No ad ran, so the page had no real visitors and I couldn’t measure the 5% target.

In a real project, I would ask the owner for the price, a course photo and a review from a company that took the course. Then I would run a small ad campaign and A/B test the headline once visitors arrive.

AI use.
I set the goal, built and styled the page in HubSpot, and chose the photos. Claude helped plan the structure, suggested copy variants and checked colour contrast and the published page.
