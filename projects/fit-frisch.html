Fit & Frisch: from sign-up to repeat order

A landing page and a HubSpot email journey for a fictional Berlin lunch brand: office workers sign up, confirm their email, get a free-delivery code, and receive reminders until their first order and beyond.

Part 1: The landing page

Scenario

You work for Fit & Frisch, a small healthy meal-prep chain. The owner says: "We want more customers and to become better known this autumn. Put together a campaign!"

Campaign goal - "Back to Work"

Generate 150 qualified leads (email addresses submitted via a landing page) from Berlin office workers in Mitte, Kreuzberg, and Prenzlauer Berg between 1–14 September 2026, via an Instagram and Google ads campaign offering a free-delivery promo code redeemable on their first order (one code per customer, valid for 14 days after they confirm their email).

How I built it

  1. Goal and plan with ClaudeI defined the campaign goal, audience and offer, and planned the page sections with Claude.
  2. First build with ClaudeClaude built the page for one scenario as a single HTML file.
  3. My edits in VS CodeI reworked the structure, rewrote the copy, and changed the colours with VS Code.

The landing page

Hero

“Back at your desk. Back to eating well.” One button for the code, and an inbox card that previews the email.

How it works

Three steps:

  • drop your email
  • confirm it and get your code
  • order lunch within 14 days

Menu + final call

A preview of four dishes, then the email form with a consent line. The fine print sets the terms: sign up by 14 September, code valid for 14 days after confirmation, one code per customer.

Part 2: The email journey

Turn sign-ups into first orders, and first orders into repeat customers.

What I’d change in production

  • The shop generates each code and sends it to HubSpot.
  • The order confirmation goes out as a transactional email.
  • Orders sync by email address, and the code is tied to the sign-up address, so an order with another address still stops the reminders.
  • The form closes on 14 September; the workflows run until early October.

AI use.

  • Landing page: AI-assisted. Claude built the first version; I restructured it and rewrote the copy.
  • Email copy: AI-assisted. Drafted with Claude, edited by me.
  • Food photos: AI-generated with Google Flow.
  • HubSpot setup and testing: done by me.
