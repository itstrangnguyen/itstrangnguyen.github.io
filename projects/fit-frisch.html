Part 1 · Capture the lead: the landing page

Hero

“Back at your desk. Back to eating well.” One button for the code, and an inbox card that previews the email.

How it works

Three steps:

  • drop your email
  • confirm it and get your code
  • order lunch within 14 days

Menu + final call

A preview of four dishes, then the email form with a consent line. The fine print sets the terms: sign up by 14 September, code valid for 14 days after confirmation, one code per customer.

The form is where the page hands over to HubSpot. Continue to Part 2 ↓

Part 2 · Turn leads into orders: the email journey

It starts the moment someone submits the form. Nine emails in two HubSpot workflows turn sign-ups into first orders, and first orders into repeat orders.

What I’d change in production

  • The shop creates a unique code per contact and sends it to HubSpot.
  • The order confirmation (FF 06) goes out as a transactional email.
  • Orders sync to HubSpot by code, not only by email, so an order placed with another address still stops the reminders.
  • The free delivery applies to a customer's first order, checked at checkout (not in the CRM) by delivery address and payment method. One person with several email aliases still gets one free delivery.
  • The form closes on 14 September; the workflows run until early October.
