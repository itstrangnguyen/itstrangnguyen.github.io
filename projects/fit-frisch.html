Fit & Frisch · Back to Work

About this project. Fit & Frisch is a fictional brand created for a course task. The offer, menu and the “500+ Berlin offices” figure on the page are invented for the scenario.

The brief

After the summer break, office workers in Mitte, Kreuzberg and Prenzlauer Berg return to their desks and stop meal-prepping. Fit & Frisch delivers healthy lunches to offices.

The campaign goal: collect email addresses. The offer: free delivery on the first order, valid 1–14 September 2026.

How I built it

  1. Goal and plan with ClaudeI defined the campaign goal, audience and offer, and planned the page sections with Claude.
  2. First build with ClaudeClaude built the page for one scenario as a single HTML file.
  3. My edits in VS CodeI reworked the structure, rewrote the copy, and changed the colours by hand.

The page

Hero

“Back at your desk. Back to eating well.” One button for the code, and an inbox card that previews the email.

How it works

Three steps: drop your email, get your code, order lunch. “Ten seconds now. That's the whole commitment.”

Why now

Ties the offer to the end of summer, with a social-proof figure.

Menu + final call

A preview of four dishes, then the email form with the offer dates in the fine print.

AI use. AI-assisted: Claude helped plan the page and wrote the first version of the code. I set the goal and offer, and edited the structure, copy and colours myself.
